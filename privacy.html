Get a Grip Household: privacy

Last updated 25 November 2026

Get a Grip Household is a small shared to-do app for two people. It is a demonstration for now. This page says plainly where your information is kept and who can see it.

Your personal tasks stay on your own device

Anything you add as a personal task is kept only in the browser on the device you typed it on. It is never sent to the shared household list, to the household store, or to anyone else. A PIN on the device stops a casual tap from showing it, but it is a curtain, not a lock: the tasks are still held in the browser, so keep the device itself private.

The household list is shared

The jobs you plan together (the wall of notes, who each job is for, their steps, details and due dates, and the regular payments you bring in) make up the household list. A copy is kept on each linked device, and one shared copy is kept in a small store run on Cloudflare, under a random household code. Anyone who has the household code can read and change the list, so share it only with the person you live with. The store belongs to the app’s owner, who can technically see what it holds. Please do not put passwords, PINs, payment card numbers or bank account details in it. Supplier references such as a policy or customer number are fine if you choose to add them, because renewing usually needs them.

Reminders

If you turn on reminders on a phone, the notification address that your phone’s browser gives the app is stored in the same shared store, together with whether it belongs to Lauren or Josh. It is used only to send the reminders you asked for, a week and a day before a job’s due date. Turn reminders off in Settings (the Reminders tab), or unlink the device, and it is removed.

Regular payments and your email

The app never reads your email. The Regular payments step only gives you a prompt that you can paste into an AI of your own choosing that can read your Gmail. You check the list it produces and bring a file back into the app. What you give the AI, and what it keeps, is between you and that service.

What else is loaded

The page loads its typefaces from Google Fonts when it opens, which tells Google that a page was visited. There are no accounts, no advertising and no analytics. The app does not sign in to Google.

Removing things

  • Unlink a device in Settings (the Link tab) and it stops sharing. Its own copy stays until you clear the site’s data in the browser.
  • “Reset this device” (offered if you forget the PIN) removes the PIN and the personal tasks kept on that device.
  • To have the shared household list deleted from the store, ask the app’s owner.

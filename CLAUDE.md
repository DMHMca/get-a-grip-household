# Get a Grip Household: project brief for Claude Code

Read this first. It is the sister project of `DMHMca/get-a-grip` (the owner's personal to-do app). Keep the two separate: household work happens here; changes to Get a grip itself happen in that repository.

## THE AGREED PLAN (21 November 2026, owner's decision): A FORK OF GET A GRIP

The household app becomes a FORK of Get a grip personal: a one-off copy taken now, after which the two apps go their own ways. Nothing is kept in step between them (the owner: his personal changes may not suit Lauren and Josh, and he does not need household features himself). Josh, an experienced developer, may take the code over later. Never change the get-a-grip repository for household work.

The finished app: all of Get a grip's task handling (Today, Big, Small, activities, subtasks, Wait, details, Refresh), plus the household parts (wall, Share out, shared store). Each person's PERSONAL tasks stay private on their own devices and are never sent to the shared store; HOUSEHOLD tasks live in the shared store so both see them. Same address as now.

WHERE IT STANDS (22 Nov 2026): Stages 1 to 4 are BUILT and live together (first release, version H1, sw cache `gagh-v4`). How it is put together:

- `index.html` is the PLANNING side (the old household app, kept): it always OPENS ON "1 · Brainstorm" (owner). - `tasks.html` is Get a grip (the fork, unchanged apart from below): Today, Big, Small, Master, Activities and every edit screen. Its own storage keys start `gagh.` (the `LS` wrapper). Header: "‹ Plan together" (back to `index.html`) and "<name>’s day".
- HOUSEHOLD LINK (in `tasks.html`, block "HOUSEHOLD LINK"): jobs Share out gave to this device's person (`household.local.v2` `me`), and their steps, appear as ordinary tasks in the activity "Household" (id `hh`). ONLY tasks in the Household activity are ever written to the shared document. `hh.mirror` (prefixed) remembers which ids came from the household.
- WHICH TASKS TO SHOW (owner's request): Everything / Household / Personal buttons on Today, Big, Small and Master (`getScope`, `inScope`, saved per device). - Branch `fork` holds the bare Stage 1 copy (commit `bc7360c`); no longer needed.

Still to do: Stage 3's "set up" is done through Get a grip's own edit screen on the phone (open a Household task); a dedicated set-up step on the planning side has not been built. Personal-task sync between a person's own devices via Cloudflare (see NO GOOGLE DRIVE) is not built. Stage 5 (handover) not done.

"This device is" gets a third choice, "Shared screen" (for a PC they share): it shows only household things and never offers personal tasks. Tap a job for the detail panel.
- PRIORITY: each person's column IS their priority order (top first). Drag up or down within a column to reorder; sideways hands the job over. The order is shared (a rank field on household tasks) and carries into that person's Big and Small lists on their phone; reordering on the phone updates the column. Personal tasks are dragged in among household jobs on the phone without disturbing the household jobs' order among themselves. Due within a week still goes to the top of Today.

AREAS ARE ACTIVITIES (owner, 22 Nov 2026): on the phone each planning area is its own household activity, id `hh:<area key>` (`isHH`, `hhActFor`, names with the area's emoji in `HH_AREAS`, custom areas from `cats`). Moving a job between household activities changes its `area`; moving it into a personal activity takes it out of the household. The old single 'hh' activity is migrated away on load. The detail panel has an Area choice.

DEVICE PIN (owner, 22 Nov 2026, after being told a PIN is only a curtain): personal tasks belong to the DEVICE, so once its owner sets a PIN (Sharing tab, or on first "Household together"), it is asked before leaving Household together or Shared screen, or switching the device to the other person (`needPin`, `askMove`, `applyMove`, `pinPanel`). Salted SHA-256 in `household.local.v2` (`pin`, `pinSalt`, `pinWho`), never synced. Five wrong tries = 30 s wait. "Reset this device" removes the PIN AND every `gagh.` key (the personal tasks), so a forgotten PIN never exposes anything.

DUE DATES AND REMINDERS (owner, 22 Nov 2026; both Lauren and Josh use Android). Household jobs may carry `due` (YYYY-MM-DD) and `rep:'year'`, set in Get a grip's edit sheet (`dueRow`, Household tasks only; personal tasks have no due date). The date is deliberately QUIET (owner: no obvious due dates, no pile of missed ones): it shows only in the edit sheet, never in lists, and there is no overdue marking. Instead: (1) the Cloudflare store sends a phone notification a week before and the day before, only to the phones of the person the job belongs to; (2) a job due within 7 days (or with a step due within 7 days) goes to the TOP of Today's Automatic and Refresh choices (`dueSoon`, `dueFirst` in `smallPicks` and `bigCandidates`); (3) finishing a repeating job makes next year's copy (`rolled:true` on the old one; done in `hhPush` and in the planning page's `rollRepeats`). Phones sign up on the Sharing tab ("Turn on reminders", needs a household code); `L.push` holds the endpoint; changing "This device is" moves the sign-up; "Send a test reminder" checks it. `sw.js` handles `push` (fetches the text from the store's `/msg`) and `notificationclick` (opens `tasks.html`).

NO GOOGLE DRIVE (owner, 21 Nov 2026): Drive sync keeps asking to sign in, so it is hidden in this app (the Sync line is removed from the More menu; the code is still there, unused). Syncing a person's PERSONAL tasks between their own devices should go through the Cloudflare store instead, under a separate private personal code that is never the household code, so no approval is needed on every start. Not yet built; fit it in alongside Stage 2 or 4 and confirm the design with the owner first.

NO "BOTH" (owner, 21 Nov 2026): every job is assigned to ONE person, never both ("a guarantee that each of them will leave it to the other"). When Share out comes across in Stage 2, drop its Both column; a job is Lauren's or Josh's.

Open questions (ask when they arise): keep made-up example notes while it is a demo? The old household app in `get-a-grip/household/` stays for now (owner, 21 Nov 2026).

- Live: https://dmhmca.github.io/get-a-grip-household/ (GitHub Pages, branch `main`, root): `index.html` (planning) plus `tasks.html` (Get a grip; APP_VERSION `H1`, bump each release), all storage keys prefixed `gagh.` through the `LS` wrapper, Drive file `get-a-grip-household-data.json`, `sw.js` cache `gagh-vNN`. - `index.html`: the whole app. `sw.js`: cache `gagh-vNN` (bump on every release; it only deletes caches starting `gagh-`, so it never touches Get a grip's caches). `manifest.webmanifest`: name "Get a Grip Household", plum icons in this folder.
- Storage: `household.doc.v2` (shared document: `tasks`, `gone`, `day`, `cats`) and `household.local.v2` (this device: `code`, `me`, `tab`, `stage`, `idx`, `area`, `part`, `walkOpen`, `welcomed`, `local`).
- Shared store: Cloudflare Worker `household-store` on the OWNER's Cloudflare account, `https://household-store.dmhmca-apps.workers.dev` (constant `SERVER`), code in `worker/worker.js`, KV namespace `household-demo` bound as `HOUSEHOLD`. `GET /h/<CODE>` returns `{v,doc}`; `PUT /h/<CODE>` with `{base,doc}` saves only if `base` equals the stored version (else 409 with the current copy). CORS allows `https://dmhmca.github.io`. Since 22 Nov 2026 also: `POST /sub/<CODE>`, `/unsub/<CODE>`, `/test/<CODE>`, `/msg` for reminders, and a daily cron `7 8 * * *` (UTC) that sends them (details at the top of `worker/worker.js`). Pushes are VAPID-signed: public key in `worker.js` and `index.html` (`VAPID_PUBLIC`), private key ONLY as the Worker secret `VAPID_PRIVATE` (never commit it; if lost, make a new pair, update both public copies, and every phone must turn reminders on again). Deploy with the Cloudflare API: upload the module with bindings `HOUSEHOLD` (KV) and keep the secret (`keep_bindings:['secret_text']`). The Cloudflare API token used to deploy expires 20 December 2026; to redeploy after that, ask the owner for a new token through the secure credential form (Edit Cloudflare Workers template, account resources = his account, zone = all zones, 30 days).
- Sync in the app: on open, on return, 1.2 s after a change, every 20 s; `merge` takes the later `updated` per task, honours `gone` tombstones (90 days), merges `day` and `cats` by key; `stable` compares documents to avoid needless writes.

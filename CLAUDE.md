# Get a Grip Household: project brief for Claude Code

Read this first. It is the sister project of `DMHMca/get-a-grip` (the owner's personal to-do app). Keep the two separate: household work happens here; changes to Get a grip itself happen in that repository.

## Get a grip's task rules (reference only)

Get a grip keeps a short document of its task model and rules: `TASK-MODEL.md` in `DMHMca/get-a-grip` (https://github.com/DMHMca/get-a-grip/blob/main/TASK-MODEL.md; read it with `git show origin/main:TASK-MODEL.md` in a clone). It is a REFERENCE ONLY: this app is a fork and goes its own way, so nothing in it binds us. Borrow a rule only when the owner agrees. ADOPTED (owner, 24 Nov 2026): rule 2 (Big/Small is never changed for you: dropping a job onto another, or adding a step, no longer makes it Big); rule 4 (a job put under another becomes its LAST step and takes its area and owner); rule 6 (a job with steps dropped onto another, or "Move its steps into a big job" in the set-up panel, or "Move its subtasks into a big task" on the phone, hands its steps over in order with ticks, appends its details to the receiving job's details, and is removed); "Part of a big job" drop-down in the set-up panel and "Part of a big task" in the phone's edit sheet (None = stand alone again); rule 7 on the planning side too (`autoFinish`: a SMALL job finishes itself when its last step is ticked and reopens if one is unticked). HOUSEHOLD TWIST: on the phone the drop-downs offer only big tasks from the SAME side (personal with personal, household with household), so nothing is shared or unshared by accident. Planning: `nest`, `unnest`, `autoFinish`, panel fields `partof` / `mergeinto`. Phone: `partSel`. Where we deliberately differ (24 Nov 2026): quiet due dates with reminders (their rule 1 says none), and priority as a shared list order (`r`), which fits their "order is the list" rule.

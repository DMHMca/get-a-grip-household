**What it is.** One app for Lauren and Josh, a FORK of the owner's personal Get a grip (the two never share code or data). Plan the household together, share every job out to ONE person, then each does a little a day. Demo, made-up data, until the owner says otherwise. **Files.** `index.html` = the PLANNING side (Brainstorm, Share out, and the Settings page). `tasks.html` = GETTING A GRIP (the doing side): Get a grip's own code (Today, Big, Small, Master, Activities, every edit screen) plus the "HOUSEHOLD LINK" that brings the person's household jobs in and sends changes back. `sw.js` (cache `gagh-vNN`; no-cache page fetches; push and notificationclick for reminders), `manifest.webmanifest`, `privacy.html` (rewritten for this app 25 Nov), `help.html` (the in-app HELP page, 25 Nov, see below), `worker/worker.js` (Cloudflare Worker), `tests/` (see below), icons.

**The flow.** (1) **Brainstorm**, five rounds: Warm up; Walk each area (tiles: House, Cleaning, Garden, Major bills, Money, Car, Kids, Health, Business ventures, Trips & fun, Paperwork, plus "Add your own"); The year ahead; **Regular payments** (a prompt for an AI that reads Gmail, then bring in its recurring.md and set each item up: whose job, small or big, when it comes into the list); **Group, sort and add details** (a two-column Big / Small board; the order IS the priority, `r`; drop a job on the middle of another to make it a step; tap a job for the set-up panel). (2) **Share out**: columns Still to share out, Lauren, Josh (no Both). (3) **Getting a grip**: the doing side, on `tasks.html`. Top bar: 1 · Brainstorm, 2 · Share out, 3 · Getting a grip; header buttons ⌂ Home and ⋮ Settings (icons only on a phone).

**Devices and layout (the taxonomy).** Each device says once whether it is a PHONE (`L.kind='phone'`: for DOING; opens on Getting a grip; the header shows "Getting a grip · <name>" and ⋮; planning is tucked behind "Planning" in that menu; ⌂ Home on the planning pages goes back to Getting a grip) or a COMPUTER (for PLANNING TOGETHER; opens on Brainstorm). Asked by a card on the first open (together with "Whose is it?"), changeable in Settings, This device tab. "This device is" = Lauren or Josh. **A PHONE NEVER HAS Household view, person chips or a PIN** (H1.11, owner: a phone stuck behind a PIN could not reach its personal tasks): `hhOnly()` and `needPin()` are false for `L.kind==='phone'`, a stored `together` is cleared on load, `tasks.html` does not bounce a phone back to the planning side, and the planning header on a phone is just ⌂, ⋮, the logo and the three tabs. There is no shared computer: only Lauren's and Josh's own devices.

One day at a time. A short menu, not a list of hundreds of things that makes you give up. Do a reasonable day’s work of both, every day.

Big or small? Decide later. Drop a small task onto a big one to make it a step, or take a step out to stand alone.

The other PC

Why a code?

There are no accounts or passwords. The code is the address and the key of your list in the shared database. Anyone who has it can read the list, so keep it between the two of you.

You never type it

Scan a QR code on a phone, or email the link to a second PC. Each device then shows only its own owner’s tasks, and changes flow both ways in about twenty seconds.

THE KEY IDEA

It looks different, but it is one app

One piece of code, one web address

Each device is told once: phone or computer, and whose it is.

A PC

Planning, and your Getting a grip. One button switches between them.

The other PC

The same. Either PC can be the planning PC.

A phone

Opens on Getting a grip. The big planning screens are tucked away.

Hidden, not different

You do not always want the big brainstorming screens. The app hides what each device does not need, from the same code.

Swap planning PC any time

Whoever has a PC open can plan. Both share the same list.

DEMO: STEP 1 OF 3

The planning PC

Open this address in Chrome:

dmhmca.github.io/get-a-grip-household

1

In the top row, press Computer.

2

Show how the wall fills, round by round: what is on your mind, walk each area, business ventures, the year ahead, regular payments.

3

Sort the notes into big and small tasks. Then Share out: give a few tasks to each of them.

DEMO: STEP 2 OF 3

Set up one phone

The same address, on the phone:

dmhmca.github.io/get-a-grip-household

1

Open that address in Chrome on the phone.

2

In the top row press Phone, then tap the phone owner’s name.

3

Chrome’s menu (three dots), Add to Home screen, so it opens like an app.

4

It opens on Getting a grip. It is empty until it is linked: that is next.

DEMO: STEP 3 OF 3

Link the phone with the QR code

1

On the PC: the three dots at the far right, Settings, the Link tab, then Create a household code. A QR code appears.

2

On the phone: point the camera at the QR code and open the link. The phone links itself.

3

On the phone open Getting a grip and press Household. Its owner’s tasks arrive within about twenty seconds.

4

Tick one. Watch it change on the PC.

5

A second PC? Press Email the link under the QR code and open it there.

Then try it for a week and let me know if it works.
